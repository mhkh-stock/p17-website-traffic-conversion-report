# P17 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will analyse your website traffic and tell you what converts

**What the client gets:** a traffic & conversion report for their
online store — where visitors come from, which channel actually
brings buyers, where the funnel leaks, which pages and devices
convert, and three prioritised fixes with the money attached. Delivered
as a 2-page PDF report plus a live Excel workbook they can re-run on
next month's GA4 export themselves.

## Tiers (Hashir's band)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | The 2-page PDF report on 1 month of their GA4 data: channel table, funnel, top landing pages |
| Standard | $65 | 3 days | Everything in Basic + 6 months of history, monthly trend + weekend vs weekday, device split, the live Excel workbook with formulas |
| Premium | $140 | 5 days | Everything in Standard + 12 months of history, channel-by-month breakdown, the 3 recommendations with revenue estimates, a reusable report template for their own future exports, 1 revision round |

**What's NOT included:** setting up GA4 or fixing their tracking
(quoted separately if their data is broken); running or managing
their ads (that's a different service); a Power BI dashboard (that's
the P3 service).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads their
GA4 export, not from order placement.

**Proof this service works:** this repo's sample — CityStore.pk,
202,793 sessions over 6 months, PKR 12,910,349 revenue, 2.21%
overall conversion, Paid Search converting best at 3.00% against
Social's 1.13%, the view → cart leak found at 12.07%, mobile at
69.1% of sessions converting worse than desktop, 140 automated
checks passing, and a 2-page PDF the buyer sees before ordering.
